SECTION 10: SCALE INCONSISTENCIES AND CONCERNS
===============================================

1. TERRITORY POSITION ANOMALY:
   Territory .tscn files contain WORLD positions (e.g., 526.45, 142.38)
   These are treated as absolute coordinates, then scaled by 19x Continents parent
   Result: implicit double-scaling of positions

2. CONTINENT SCALE VARIANCE:
   Continents have different scales (0.13 to 0.255) within the 19x parent
   Creates non-uniform territorial sizing across continents
   Design intent unclear: visual layout vs. world standardization

3. MULTI-LEVEL SCALE COMPOSITION:
   Four levels of scaling before geometry:
   - Continents 19x
   - Continent instance (0.13-0.26)
   - Territory instance (0.82-2.0)
   - Territory mesh (0.12-0.40)
   Makes world-scale prediction difficult

4. UNIT SCALE COMPENSATION:
   Units use inverse scaling, disconnecting their world size from territorial size
   Keeps units visually uniform but loses relationship to world scale
   Makes "1 game unit = 1 meter" standardization complex

5. CAMERA VIEW BOUNDS (CameraController.gd lines 12-13):
   min_bounds: Vector3(-50, 5, -50)
   max_bounds: Vector3(50, 50, 50)
   These bounds are in Map-local coordinates, much smaller than territory positions
   Indicates camera operates in a local framing system, not world coordinates

SECTION 11: "1 GAME UNIT = 1 METER" STANDARDIZATION
====================================================

CURRENT SCALE REFERENCES:
  Panther tank model: real-world ~6.9m, collision shape 5.46 units long
  T34 tank model: real-world ~6.7m, collision shape 0.41 units long
  Unit scale: 2.0 (applied to both)
  
  At these scales, 1 game unit ≠ 1 meter (would need units ~6.7-6.9 long)

PROBLEM: The nested scale composition (19 * 0.13-0.26 * 0.82-2.0 * 0.12-0.40)
creates effective scales ranging from ~0.3 to ~3.0 for territories, making
world unit standardization impossible without refactoring.

REQUIRED FOR STANDARDIZATION:
1. Define root Map scale factor that converts game units to real meters
2. Eliminate continent and territory scale variance (use 1.0 for all)
3. Rescale all territory meshes uniformly
4. Adjust camera bounds to world coordinates
5. Recalibrate projectile max_distance
6. Document final scale ratio (e.g., "1 unit = 0.5 meters")
